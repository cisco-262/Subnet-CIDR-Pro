SUBNET CIDR PRO

Privacy Policy

Subnet CIDR Pro is designed for local, on-device network calculations. Your addresses and VLSM plans are not sent to OpsHome.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

Subnet CIDR Pro calculates IPv4 networks, subnet masks, wildcard masks, address ranges and VLSM plans. It also provides a built-in CIDR reference.

2. On-device calculations

Values you enter and results generated by the app are processed on your device. The app does not require an OpsHome account or a backend service for calculations.

3. Data we do not collect

  • No account registration is required.
  • No entered IP address, subnet, host requirement or VLSM plan is uploaded to OpsHome.
  • The app contains no advertising SDK or analytics SDK.
  • The app does not request network device credentials, passwords or private keys.

4. Clipboard access

When you tap Paste, the app reads the current clipboard value so it can place that value in the address field. When you tap a copy action, the selected result is written to the clipboard. The app does not read the clipboard in the background.

5. External links

If you open an App Store, website, email or social link from the app, your device connects to that external service. Its own privacy policy applies. Network calculations do not require opening an external link.

6. Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the related feature is released.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@opshome.run.
