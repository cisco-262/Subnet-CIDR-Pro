FAQ

Clear answers before you install.

Does Subnet CIDR Pro work without an internet connection?

Yes. IPv4, CIDR, VLSM planning and the built-in reference run on your device. No sign-in or backend service is required for calculations.

Is there a subscription or in-app purchase?

No. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads or analytics SDKs. App Store pricing and any temporary promotions are shown on Apple's listing.

Which Apple devices are supported?

Subnet CIDR Pro is designed for iPhone and requires iOS 16 or later. See the App Store compatibility section for the latest device details.

How does the VLSM planner work?

Add named subnet requirements and host counts within a parent IPv4 network. The planner allocates larger blocks first and warns when the address space is insufficient.

Are /31 and /32 prefixes supported?

Yes. The calculator covers /0–/32, including RFC 3021 point-to-point /31 networks and /32 single-address host routes.
